VENDOR SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Agreement Date: February 1, 2025
Agreement Number: VSA/2025/001

BETWEEN:
XYZ Manufacturing Ltd., having its registered office at 789 Industrial Area, 
Pune, Maharashtra 411001 (hereinafter referred to as the "Buyer")

AND:
Quality Supplies Pvt. Ltd., having its registered office at 321 Commerce Hub, 
Mumbai, Maharashtra 400001 (hereinafter referred to as the "Vendor")

1. SCOPE OF SUPPLY
The Vendor agrees to supply raw materials including steel sheets, aluminum rods, 
and copper wires as per specifications in Annexure A. All materials must meet 
IS standards and quality requirements.

2. CONTRACT VALUE AND PRICING
Total Contract Value: INR 50,00,000 (Fifty Lakhs Rupees)
Unit prices are fixed for 12 months from the date of this agreement.
GST @ 18% shall be charged separately as applicable.

3. DELIVERY TERMS
Delivery Location: Buyer's warehouse at Pune
Delivery Schedule: As per monthly purchase orders
Delivery Time: Within 15 days of purchase order
The Vendor shall bear all transportation and insurance costs until delivery.

4. PAYMENT TERMS
Payment shall be made within 45 days of receipt of invoice and satisfactory 
delivery. A 2% early payment discount is available for payments within 15 days.
Late payments shall attract interest at 18% per annum.

5. QUALITY ASSURANCE
All materials must meet specified quality standards. The Buyer reserves the 
right to inspect and reject non-conforming materials within 7 days of delivery.
Rejected materials shall be replaced at the Vendor's cost within 10 days.

6. WARRANTIES
The Vendor warrants that all materials shall be free from defects for 12 months 
from delivery. The Vendor shall replace defective materials at no additional cost.

7. PENALTY CLAUSE
Late delivery penalty: 1% of order value per week, maximum 10%
Quality rejection penalty: 5% of rejected material value
Repeated quality failures may result in contract termination.

8. INDEMNITY
The Vendor shall indemnify the Buyer against all claims, losses, and damages 
arising from defective materials or breach of this Agreement, including 
consequential damages and loss of profit.

9. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice.
The Buyer may terminate immediately if the Vendor fails to meet quality 
standards on three consecutive occasions.
Upon termination, all pending orders shall be completed.

10. FORCE MAJEURE
Neither party shall be liable for delays due to acts of God, natural disasters, 
war, government actions, or circumstances beyond reasonable control. The 
affected party must notify within 48 hours.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality of pricing, specifications, and 
business information for 3 years after contract termination.

12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
The Vendor's total liability shall not exceed the total contract value. Neither 
party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Disputes shall first be resolved through negotiation. Unresolved disputes shall 
be referred to arbitration in Mumbai under Indian Arbitration Act.

14. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Courts in Mumbai shall have 
exclusive jurisdiction.

15. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between parties and 
supersedes all prior negotiations and agreements.
