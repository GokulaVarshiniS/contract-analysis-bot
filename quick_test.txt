SERVICE CONTRACT

Date: January 1, 2025

Between: ABC Company Ltd (Client)
And: XYZ Services Pvt Ltd (Provider)

1. SERVICES: Provider shall deliver software development services.

2. PAYMENT: Client shall pay INR 5,00,000 within 30 days of invoice.

3. PENALTY: Late payment attracts 2% monthly interest. Provider may 
suspend services if payment is delayed beyond 60 days.

4. TERMINATION: Either party may terminate with 30 days notice. Client 
shall pay for all work completed. Provider may terminate immediately 
if payment is not received within 90 days.

5. INDEMNITY: Client shall indemnify Provider against all claims and 
shall hold Provider harmless from any liability.

6. LIMITATION: Provider's total liability shall not exceed fees paid.

7. NON-COMPETE: Client shall not hire Provider's employees for 1 year.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY: Both parties shall maintain confidentiality for 5 years.

9. JURISDICTION: Courts of Mumbai, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
Disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration.

Signed by both parties on the date first written above.
