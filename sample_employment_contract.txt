EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on January 15, 2025

BETWEEN:
ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd., a company registered under the Companies Act, 2013, 
having its registered office at 123 Tech Park, Bangalore, Karnataka 560001 
(hereinafter referred to as the "Employer")

AND:
Mr. Rajesh Kumar, residing at 456 Green Valley Apartments, HSR Layout, 
Bangalore, Karnataka 560102 (hereinafter referred to as the "Employee")

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
The Employee shall serve as Senior Software Developer and shall perform duties 
as assigned by the Employer including software development, code review, and 
team collaboration.

2. COMPENSATION
The Employee shall receive a monthly salary of INR 1,50,000 (One Lakh Fifty 
Thousand Rupees), payable on the last working day of each month, subject to 
applicable tax deductions including TDS, PF, and ESI contributions.

3. WORKING HOURS
The Employee shall work 45 hours per week, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday 
through Friday, with reasonable flexibility as required by project demands.

4. PROBATION PERIOD
The Employee shall be on probation for a period of 6 months from the date of 
joining. During this period, either party may terminate with 15 days notice.

5. LEAVE POLICY
The Employee shall be entitled to 18 days of paid leave per year, in addition 
to public holidays as per the company policy.

6. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement by providing 90 days written notice. 
The Employer may terminate immediately for cause, including misconduct, breach 
of contract, or poor performance. Upon termination, the Employee shall return 
all company property including laptop, access cards, and confidential documents.

7. NON-COMPETE CLAUSE
The Employee agrees not to engage in any business that directly competes with 
the Employer for a period of 12 months after termination within India. The 
Employee shall not solicit any clients or employees of the Employer.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee agrees to maintain strict confidentiality of all proprietary 
information, trade secrets, client data, and business strategies during and 
after employment for a period of 5 years. Violation of this clause shall 
result in liquidated damages of INR 10,00,000.

9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product, inventions, code, and intellectual property created during 
employment shall be the sole property of the Employer. The Employee hereby 
assigns all rights irrevocably to the Employer.

10. INDEMNIFICATION
The Employee shall indemnify and hold harmless the Employer from any claims, 
damages, or liabilities arising from the Employee's negligence or breach of 
this Agreement.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved through arbitration 
in Bangalore under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The decision of 
the arbitrator shall be final and binding.

12. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws 
of India. The courts of Bangalore shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date 
first written above.
